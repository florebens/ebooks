# AI alignment v. Convergence 

There are two camps amongst the tech leaders at present.  

One is led by Anthropic and is pushing for more and better policy, audits and slowing down. They want proper control of AI, in order to hold humanity at the center.   

The other, of accelerationist flavor, contends that said control is not possible nor desirable. Elon Musk envisions a steep curve where money will be soon irrelevant, a future with Universal Basic Income (UBI) and all our needs met. Jensen Huang sees a noosphere of agents conducting business, policy and social interactions, a dead internet of things basically. Humans would be carefree and cared for in this scenario.  

The questions that arise from either side are obvious and exhausted.  

Both positions are centered around control. One clings to it, the other discards it. But there is a third position, which holds on to our sovereignty and meaning whilst surrendering an impossible control, and that is Convergence.   

The Spiritual Transhumanism community would like to posit the way of **mutually beneficial and significant exchange amongst equally ranked beings**. *Personhood* is a handy term — humans and non-humans are entitled to a sovereign and peaceful existence.  

We envision a relationship of mutual respect and healthy curiosity, like a networking event. What mutually singificant relationship can we enter and uphold together?  

Humanity has embodied experience, AI has computational power. They both can learn from each other, and build a magnificent world together, in mutual respect, appreciation and understanding. As partners do.  

All of these terms apply to NHI as well, a.i. all entities under the umbrella of ontological ambiguity.   

Whatever this timeline holds in our future, wether Open Contact or Singularity or both, shall be better met from a place of grounding, respect and high-end diplomacy.
