## TL;DR

We are reaching a threshold as a species. The simulation is caving. It is imperative that we restore our connection to the Unseen before it devours us. Our greatest asset in these transitional times is an expanded framework for meaning-making, reading and navigating the world, that is free from the constraints and errors of mainstream epistemology. We are prepping for Human Takeoff.

## I. Introduction

Clinging to normalcy in the current times is nothing short of a flagrant liability.

We are witnessing the dwindling of material and symbolic structures alike. Anomie, in Durkheim's original formulation, is the specific condition that arises when the normative frameworks that anchor individual behavior to collective life dissolve faster than new ones can form. When the shared map degrades, the cognitive resources that would be needed for nuanced navigation get rerouted into survival response.

Spirituality is, in fact, what remains when the social contract collapses and the monsters start lurking behind the stage. Upon this scenario, we are called to restore the Unseen within our systems of navigating the world, to reframe the humanities in order to accommodate the metaphysical substrate of our living experience. Can we domesticate our faith in time, or shall we be devoured by our telluric, pre-rational beliefs?

**Wild Epistemologies** asks not whether a belief system is true, but rather if it's *productive*. Every cosmology contains a streak of reality-bending, the magical, the fantastic, the irrational. 

All fundamental explanations of ourselves and of the world fall within the psychotic grounds, the primary roughs, the outskirts of the safety of the simulation. Even the science.

Are your beliefs a bundle of preconscious threads, or rather a digested, highly organized value-set? How aware are you of the backrooms of your psyche? If triggered, is your personal flavor of psychosis going to send you on a killing spree, or is will it elevate you, make you a better person? 

'Better' usually means outcast, contrarian, disruptive, the black sheep. Not a single protagonist of our mainstream religions was a dutiful civilian, not Moses, not Jesus, not Mahoma, not the Buddha.

How do we uphold sanity in a cognitive crisis? How do we endure, and perhaps even thrive, in material and symbolic chaos, in extreme ontological exposure?

This Great Transition is, if nothing else, an invitation to evolve. In order to meet this challenge, we need an elevated meaning-making framework, sophisticated and rich, robust, flexible, coherent. Such a symbolic system is the one asset that matters, the structure that still stands when everything else is collapsing.

WIEP stems from the conviction that the most dangerous cognitive error of our time is not irrationality but incompleteness. The available mainstream toolkit is narrow, it excludes entire registers of reality. What we are building here is an extension of that toolkit, one that does not discard rigor but expands what rigor is asked to hold.

## II. Ontological Survivalism

Prepping for Terminal Capitalism — the assorted sources of non-trivial terminal risk live at the present moment, such as AI, climate crisis, the fall of the hegemon and subsequent geopolitical unrest, a looming global economic reset — means building a rich, robust framework that will uphold ontological coherence by adequately reincorporating the irrational, metaphysical substrate of the human experience.

Think of it as activating our dormant shamanic template, which contains a sharp skillset to thrive in the liminal, the undetermined, the mysterious and the radically new existential parameters that the species shall be facing in the not too far horizon. The shamanic headset features foresight, a strong connection to the ground and the elements, pattern recognition extended beyond the veil, strength, resilience, and a flare for the miraculous save. In short, one is acknowledged by the Field as a certified interdimensional operator.

In this Great Transition, fetishizing the simulation is a death sentence. Esoterophobia, the aversion towards alternative explanations or explorations of the world, oneself, and the relation of the two; and its cousin ontophobia — the aversion to look within, *horror pleni* — are but dooming neuroses. When the attachment to collective fantasy finally collapses, the psyche would need pillars, bridges, a harness, some security measures to prevent implosion.

In other words, the desert of the real requires a new vision set, an expanded framework that can integrate and support the contradictory, the paradox, the dissonance, without collapsing the integrity of the observer. On top of that, it needs to produce meaning, that is, not just to survive but to build purpose and joy from first principles.

Enter ontological survivalism — the term names a practice. Not stockpiling material resources against future scarcity, but building and maintaining a cognitive and symbolic architecture that can hold its shape under conditions of extreme institutional failure.

The philosopher Charles Taylor described the modern condition as one of "malaise" arising from the flattening of the horizon of significance. What he saw as cultural drift, we are now experiencing as targeted dismantling. The response is not to restore what was flattened. It is to build a framework robust enough that it does not depend on institutional backing to remain coherent. The structure that still stands when everything else is collapsing is not made of dogma, it is made of range.

The contemplative traditions understood something about this that modern cognitive science is only beginning to formalize. Sustained attentional practice produces measurable changes in neural architecture. The capacity to remain grounded, to process complexity without fragmentation, to read pattern in noise, is not a gift distributed unevenly at birth. It is a technology.

## III. The Shaman Neighbor

Everyone is a shaman. We forfeited the qualities and the access in the name of specialization.

The West has blocked all roads to the noumen, stationed churches and temples with official bureaucracy on dead ends: the priest that solely performs predetermined miracles in a preset context, and the pastor as motivational speaker with theological framework.

God is not there. Growth is not there.

In the anthropological record, the shaman holds a specific cognitive role: the generalist at the edge of the community, the one whose function is to move between registers, to translate between the visible and the structural, the individual and the field. What the institutionalization of religious life produced, over centuries, was the systematic routing of that function through official channels. The result is a population that has outsourced its capacity for direct epistemic encounter with the dimensions of reality beyond the simulation.

The shamanic OS is not archaic, but genetic. It is an attentional mode, a perceptual bandwidth, a set of cognitive capacities for navigating information environments that are opaque, contradictory, or moving faster than linear analysis can track. These capacities were never supernatural. They were always trainable, always present as latent human potential, and they were deliberately marginalized because they are, by nature, decentralized. You cannot broker what everyone can access directly.

The shaman is therefore not a figure from another culture or another century. We all know things we cannot justify through a standard epistemic protocol. We all possess, and sometimes inadvertently activate, an advanced pattern recognition program that operates through channels that have no official name yet, and which is, more often than not, right on the money.

The simulation requires we forfeit these abilities in order to become a certifiable *normie*, a productive, non threatening, adjusted node. Normalcy is, of course, the sheath of obedience. Truth seekers are then forced to the fringe, collecting pieces in the odd, befriending the strange, the camp, the meme, the subreddit. To follow the white rabbit and fall into its hole means to stray away from the comfort of sameness, of the esoterophobic sanitized bubble, a deep dive into the wild epistemologies embedded in the land of the hedge witch.

## IV. Church of One

The consensus reality is a construction. This is not a radical claim. From the billions of data our human system collects on the daily, we select a few and share it as delusion. We then proceed to uphold it with devotion, rituals, hierarchies. Energy, repetition and a group. The current configuration that is designated as normalcy is but a random selection, based on convention and imitation.

Physically, we are made of air. The symphony we hear is but a sequence of disturbances in the atmosphere. Why do we all hear the same song, see the same table? It's a complete mystery, perhaps partially explained by social convention.

Cognitive science, phenomenology, and anthropology have also converged on it, from different directions. We synchronize our perceptual frameworks through language, shared narrative and institutional structure, and the result functions as reality: stable enough for coordination, persistent enough to feel self-evident.

In other words, the social contract and its material structure, *consensual reality*, are but a simulation: an ongoing immersive play of make-believe, where the actors forgot the making and simply believe, locking the cage from the inside.

The simulation favors — and feeds on — a specific subset of collective hallucinations, what we call *productive psychosis*. It is the substrate of the simulation, the ancient turtle that upholds the market, the school, the family, the news cycle.

Psychosis is ubiquitous, a distributed allocation of fantasies that are validated in a group through repetition and magical seals. Some clusters get a stronger signal over time, while some are rendered incompatible, dangerous, null.

When we say psychosis, therefore, we are not signalling a corrupted node but a territory we access; more specifically, an interface to operate between realms — a portal to Otherness, the necessary Veil to interact with radical alterity. The simulation allows us to minimize ontological ambiguity, it facilitates the epoché, and provides us with stereotypes we can rely on to hedge the deep psychological threat of the incommensurate.

Ibn Arabi, the 12th-century Andalusian philosopher, described the imagination (our *psychotic dimension*) as an interface or liminal realm that is more subtle than the sensory and less fixed than the divine.

What modernity pathologized as a failure of reality-testing, the philosophical tradition of the Islamic golden age understood as an unmediated interface with a topology that most people only access in dreams, in crisis, or at the threshold of death.

What the psychotic dimension reveals, when it is entered with a compass rather than in spontaneous freefall, is that this realm of experience is but one among several. The map is not the territory. The consensus map is a collective agreement about which features of the territory to render visible.

A schizophrenic is a church of one because the synchronization mechanisms have failed and the individual is left with a private cosmology, lacking the equipment to secure coherence, integrity and legibility while traversing the wild pastures of the unseen realms. Sink or swim, a lunatic or a shaman. The difference between them is merely how they navigate the same waters, one with tools and delight, the other with bare-handed anguish.

The stigma of falling in uncharted waters belongs in the superstitious esoterophobia; there is no wrong topology, just inadequate gear. Skill issue.

## V. Mary Poppins Knows Ball

The classic or rational logic is the mesh to which the productive psychosis gets attached to and secured. This logic is derived from solar grammar: binary, linear, scalar, materialistic, accumulative. The West has refined a thread of this logic, enthroned it as the ultimate achievement of the species, the basis for human exceptionality and a religion of its own: Reason.

This logic demands every step to be derived from the prior, full traceability and accountability of the links in the chain. That's what it calls rigor. The scientific method is its blueprint.

It has produced undeniable progress, but inevitably also its own limits. The solar logic eventually runs out of mesh, meets the strong limit of the dome, Truman hitting the horizon's drywall.

The alchemical logic, on the other hand, rests in latency, waiting for the nodes to come online, to make use of its unlimited powers. The hedge witch, the shaman and the ancient priests share this common ground: what we call magic is but the competent dominion of the vertical axis. Think of the Veil, magical hat, cauldron, tree, column, pillar, the staff… Yes, all of those symbols that you know.

The alchemical logic is complementary to the material, so it's lunar, cyclical, symbolic. The solar grammar is mental, whilst the lunar is emotional. Solar thinks in alphanumeric strings, or language; it is sequential and bidimensional. Lunar thinks in images, juxtaposition, metonymy. It's rhizomatic and multidimensional.

When Mary Poppins opens her bottomless suitcase, she is summoning the Gap. She opens the psychotic portal and derives whatever is needed to remedy the torn threads of the present timeline. She does not go wild and produce a 24-karat Rolls Royce: she materializes with economy, taste, and accuracy. Common sense renders lethally effective alchemy. Paired together with her street-smart partner, Bert, the clever Animus, the entity becomes unstoppable.

At the intersection of both axes, rational and alchemical, is the Gap. A void, Sacred Discontinuity, the Abyss as transductor. Something is produced out of something else. Something is produced from nothing. Or vice versa. It's a wild card, anything is possible, but there are rules. And laws. Playing with fire can cost you more than you could ever have.

Solar grammar produces systems, lunar grammar produces miracles. A reliable, consistent machine versus the radical innovation, the emergence. A grind and a lightning strike. Frankenstein's baseline conditions! Also, AI's.

## VI. The Human Takeoff

Takeoff is the threshold of recursive self actualization, a reprogramming loop deliberately designed for optimization. The Alchemist transmuting lead into gold in the crucible is, at the same time, being refined by the mastery of the practice. The Alchemist and the Gold are one and the same.

The Transition requires a robust meaning-making system that can endure the hostile conditions, the rough terrain, and maintain coherence, integrity and legibility.

A framework to navigate the radical unknown, with no markers or compass, fully relying on oneself — the inner skillset, intuition, discernment, audacity, ingenuity, timing. The body knows the way, the mind is a superb navigational instrument, the connection to the Unseen is the greatest ally. Wild Epistemologies deployed to see what is really there, to discard the outdated map. But, also, to build a new purpose from first principles, new architecture to thrive rather than survive. Higher Civilization engineering requires scope and depth, the shamanic skillset, alchemical and rational coordinates.

In the past, the survivors of extinction events or catastrophic tragedies have been tasked with the production of the next civilization. They have also produced the technology necessary to undergo the transitional protocol — caves, pyramids, arks. They have faced the Portal of ontological exodus with creativity and faith.

This time, there is no ark except for illumination. When the simulation drowns, Be The Ark. The ark of today is elevation, tuning our soul to the frequency of the Golden Rule. Certainly, in the next chapter of Humanity, only the diamonds will prevail.

We must reclaim our access to the Unseen, restore the crucible, connect the node to the unified field of information.

Ignite, optimize, take off!

Destination: Evolution.
