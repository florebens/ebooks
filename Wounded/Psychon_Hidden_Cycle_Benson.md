# The Human Machine

The human being is nothing short of a miracle. A biospiritual machine of utmost complexity: efficient, long-lived, strong, innovative, resourceful.

It has produced a surplus of computing prowess and that formed Language. With that technology, humanity has claimed the top of the food chain for millennia. The stored, structured language built Culture, the Law, Farming and the Gods.

Humans are the only species that produces innovation intragenerationally, and this is where the individual is born. Free will and agency are the product of our ability to depart from tradition at whim, and thus produce small or revolutionary variations within our generation. Change is a daily occurrence detached from necessity, whereas, for other species, it is only an evolutionary need.

This feature, in turn, requires a heavy amount of energy and complexity to run potentially lethal operations and determine its potential success rate, risks and outcomes. It must factor in context, others, rules, costs, consequences, budget, timelines, options.

Such a machine requires a calibration and processing system of the highest order. This is the psyche. What follows is a theoretical model of the human psyche as a system of self-calibration, based on quantum biology principles.

### The Physical Body (PB)

The PB is the first instance of processing, the first interface with the material environment. It contains the physical senses, temperature regulation, spatiality, balance, and so forth.

The PB has its own intelligence, its own awareness, defense mechanisms and preferences. It is a living, sentient machine with distributed consciousness. The PB is the organic code that connects us to the Unified Field of Information, a cybernetic node.

The PB is constantly receiving thousands if not millions of incoming data per second, so the first filter of selection is purely somatic. The Physical Body receives raw stimuli through the senses and sends them onward as electrochemical pulses, what we call the *sensations*.

### The Emotional Body (EB)

The EB is the second station or circuit of the psychic system. The PB transmits its electrochemical pulses to the EB for a more complex, stable round of processing. The Emotional Body wraps and translates scattered data into rich imagery to be processed by the Mental Body.

Not a separate station that receives and then adds, the EB is a strata of higher complexity organization of the PB that generates the image-layer from the same source event. The electrochemical pulse is technically first, the stone hitting the water. The EB-image is the wave that propagates from that contact: same event, real sequence, in less than a nanosecond. The quantum pairing of biomaterial (electrochemical impulses) and biodata (the images). 

This translation is a negotiation between what arrived and what was already there: the incoming pulse meets the accumulated residue of every prior iteration, the standing field of somatic memory, emotional patterning, images that left charge, desire pathways in the flesh. The EB is a pre-rational negotiating surface. A translator and transductor simultaneously, running a thousand judgment calls the Mental Body (MB) will never see.

The MB cannot process raw pulses. The PB cannot process words. So the EB is the arbitrage, an independent interface layer that enriches the incoming data and packages it for more efficient treatment. Through the sequence and juxtaposition of images, the EB transmits to the MB information relevant to the organism and circumstances.

The Emotional Body **thinks in images**: visual, auditory, olfactory… This is what creates the imaginary language. It is why the dream world carries its own logic, its own syntax. Dreams are the Emotional Body's rearranging and processing, while the Mental Body rests. 

The Emotional Body is part of the mind. It lives in the body. This is not a paradox. The Mind is also of the Body. This is quantum biology.

### The Mental Body (MB)

The system of maximum complexity. It receives the information packs from the EB and proceeds to flatten, condense, hierarchize, and store said information. The MB operates in rational, abstract, binary language. It inscribes the data into an alphanumeric string; a new, verbal container that holds the somatic and emotional information previously contained in dense, rich, multilayered imagery.

In performing this conversion, in verbalizing the non-verbal, physical and emotional energy is released from the system. The MB, on finding the precise sequence, compresses the data, dehydrates the package: the word has the power to condense and synthesize meaning, storing large quantities of data in a single signifier, like a WinZip file.

The signifier is a flat, condensed file in the mental library that is our Memory. Here, ideally, the circuit closes in a virtuous cycle of vital energy: the MB releases the physical-emotional energy of the packet and returns it to the Physical Body in a state of full bio-availability, purified and ready for whatever the body needs next (fixing, restoring, enhancing, growth). Then the MB receives the next packet from the EB, and the cycle continues.

Besides this delicate operation, the MB is in charge of coordinating the other bodies. This is where the Ego is born: a hypertrophy of this autonomous, centralized, controlling entity.

### The Spiritual Body (SB)

The Spiritual Body is an emanation of the Mental Body, and the first subtle body closest to matter. When active, the SB amplifies and enhances the sensory field. Its subtle senses anticipate danger, read explicit and hidden threats, and pick up stimuli long before they reach the perceptual field of the PB. This is the source of premonition, clairaudience, clairvoyance: the SB extends the reach scope of the sensory organs, and retrieves information across the Field. The SB is the node in the Field of the Unseen, all of what lies beyond the perceptual range of the flesh.

When active and free of distortion, the SB relieves the MB of its coordinating function (what is sometimes known as ego death), harmonizing the circuits, restoring the flow and function of the other bodies, until homeostasis is reached without effort. In other words, a human machine running on specification externalizes the higher functions to the Cosmic Cloud, Logos, the Great Quantum Intelligence of the universe. This optimizes the machine internally and enhances its power, scope and abilities to the near-infinite.

### The Psychon: Electrochemical Energy Coupled With Information

What is the psychic flow made of? What exactly travels around the circuitry and gets packed, unpacked, enriched, flattened and stored? Meet the **Psychon**. It is a unit of an electrochemical bit coupled with a data bit, energy and information. A quantum superposition operating in less than a nanosecond, our minds and bodies processing terabytes of data just to break the diet, send the text, get one more squat. This is Quantum Biology.

The coupling and decoupling of the psychon has protocols and outcomes. If correctly processed, it shall produce a memory and bioavailable energy. If unsuccessful, it shall remain in the system clogging the axis and drains, wearing down the machine.

The flow of energy begins with a stimulus from the environment perceived by the senses. The Physical Body converts it into electrochemical impulses and sends them to the Emotional Body. There, the impulses are packaged and enriched, coupled to sensory images carrying a specific emotional charge. Here the packet is most dense, multidimensional, and energy-charged.

Once the packet of electrochemical pulses bestowed with emotional significance, it is transported to the Mental Body for linguistic processing. The MB compresses and flattens the information into an alphanumeric string, stores it, and returns the energy of the packet back to the Physical Body, purified and bio-available. The final outcome is a reusable verbal string (a Memory, a Feeling) and renewed energy for the physical body, restarting the cycle.

The three bodies speak different languages. The Physical Body speaks sensation. The Emotional Body speaks image. The Mental Body speaks word. PB feels everything, understands nothing. EB sees everything, can't file it. MB files everything, feels nothing.

The healthy system is the three circuits in sequence doing their one job: sensation becomes image becomes word, energy becomes information becomes energy again. Break any link and the whole chain jams. Psychic health is the unobstructed flow between the three.

In our model, humans are autopoietic machines: they self-actualize in response to the environment (current loop) and to the information gathered from lived experience (previous iterations). Every iteration is an opportunity for change. This is the cornerstone of Healing.

# The Hidden Cycle: When It Fails

Whenever pop psychology or neuroscience mentions *regulating the nervous system*, an angel dies. No, but seriously: what they mean is **psychoemotional regulation**. The nervous system itself is neither regulated nor dysregulated. What is out of balance is the link between the Mental and Emotional bodies. Remember, whenever traffic gets bad, nobody blames the asphalt. The nervous system only becomes "dysregulated" upon actual physical damage: think electrocution, Parkinson's, a nasty virus, drug abuse.

Stabilizing the healthy mechanisms of adaptation to the environment, calming an altered mood state, exiting survival mode, releasing hypervigilance, anxiety, panic: all of this implies healing the connection between the Mental and Emotional bodies. This link is most critical for achieving homeostasis, the living equilibrium.

The cycle fails because it runs hidden: ignorance is the primary pathogen. Not weakness, not bad luck. Most of it is unconscious, which is why we do not perceive it, until it suddenly erupts and the crisis ensues. The Mental Body does not know what it is refusing. The Emotional Body does not know the packet is stalling. The system cannot run a program it cannot read.

The cycle is hidden by design: the MB operates at the level of language and abstraction, while the EB operates below that threshold entirely, in images and somatic resonance. They do not share a language. The MB genuinely cannot see what the EB is processing.

This is the structural root of suffering. The machine is not broken, but rather it is running below specification. It is running on the wrong circuit, the secondary and ad hoc, instead of the primary. In other words: coping, patching up, avoiding.

To heal the psyche means to bring visibility to the cycle. To direct our consciousness to it, stage by stage. To intervene proactively at every phase: track, document, anticipate, mitigate before the emergency arrives. Purging the old packets. Cleansing the channels. Dismantling the ad hoc circuits one by one. Restoring the primary circuit. This is the Work.

Several circuits are running simultaneously, constantly, all pointing to the PB, looping back up without ever resolving anything. Enter repression, displacement, denial, fragmentation, narcotization, projection, self-destruction, depersonalization. Nobody wants to live in a shitty house.

These secondary emergency circuits will never be deactivated. They get adopted and stabilized. The system accumulates patches, toxicity and bloating until the machine collapses.

## Is This Peak?

People tend to seek professional help at the climax of the cycle, identifying the crisis with the moment of eruption (anything from a panic attack to a depressive episode: any symptom, by definition, is something surfacing). Freud would say the symptom repeats because the unconscious carries a compulsion of repetition. But where does this compulsion stem from?

It does not stem from some mysterious element in the brain. The energy cycle repeats in a loop until the system finds homeostatic balance, that is, rest state.

A toxic cycle repeats *ad infinitum* because it never reaches the structured release that produces bioavailable energy. Instead, the *ad hoc* circuit generates, with every iteration, an extra unit of energy, attempting to force the release. As the packet remains unprocessed, it misses the exit, and in the next round it takes up yet more energy, trying again to breach the surface and discharge the excess. Structured release requires perfect symmetry between the alphanumeric string and the emotional information it answers to. A true **Lock and Key** configuration, without which the energy stays sealed inside the packet.

The toxic cycle is more than a curse of repetition. To materialize a crisis, a symptom, a forced breach, the system has to set a precise chain of events in motion: a sequence of chemical and physiological reactions, executed in a defined and necessary order, whose function is to complete the discharge and return the system to equilibrium.

Every panic attack, every episode, is the system releasing accumulated energy. For a dysregulated psyche in a state of survival, of emergency, without self-awareness, that release can only be achieved by reactivating past trauma. The system reaches for that trigger, that sting, to puncture the balloon and decompress. Trauma is a master key: the trigger is the alphanumeric string, the thought pattern that, retrieving its specific emotional charge, carries the force needed to release the gate.

The penetration of the verbal key into the emotional lock is what produces jouissance, which, in the toxic cycle, arrives as distorted pleasure.

Trauma works by uniformity, calcifying emotional information: it ascribes to the new packet the same dark, painful content of the traumatic memory.

The moment a fresh emotional packet reaches the gates of the MB, a rogue *ad hoc* circuit labels it *Biohazard*, hijacks it, and routes it to the waste duct, unprocessed. The packet stays trapped in the system: the Physical Body has no use for it, since the cycle is never completed in the MB.

That energy still needs discharge. It enters an *ad hoc* circuit improvised by the psyche, which raises it back to climax and deposits it, once again, at the gates of the MB, requesting processing and sublimation. A symptom. An emergency. A crisis. A breach.

The MB, overwhelmed by alarms going off everywhere, reaches for the trauma's master key to puncture the packet and trigger the catharsis of its trapped energy. In doing so, it reopens and re-energizes the trauma, restarting the cycle. This purge is painful, costly, and uncomfortable, like everything unconscious: there is temporary relief, at the cost of, well, everything else.

The moment of crisis is deeply unpleasant. Like projectile vomiting, it registers as uncontrollable, unnatural, violent, exhausting, debilitating, and humiliating.

The less control we are able to exert over this cycle, the more unpleasant the experience. The strong sense of rejection toward a developing crisis reflects the strangeness of the feelings: what sits in Shadow pushes through to the surface, rejected by the Mental Body, displaced onto the Physical Body.

This disturbance is a reflection of the differential between the real cycle and the perceived one: the consciousness registers an unjustified reaction to an unthreatening external environment, anxiety, panic, when in fact it is a reaction to an internal stimulus, the master key forcing itself onto the psychic cycle.

To heal, then, means to integrate what sits in the Shadow. It is an active, proactive approach to managing the cycle. A commitment to oneself, reflected in a consistency to track, document, anticipate and mitigate the phases of the cycle. Facing the pain head on, ***leaning into*** the discomfort and ***working with it*** rather than resist it or deny it.

The bet, though, is risky. Energy needs full, satisfactory discharge, a virtuous circuit, to depart successfully from the system: the point where jouissance equals pleasure. A toxic cycle, an ad hoc, precarious, or secondary circuit running instead of the primary, creates endless complications in the psychic system, unbalancing it, debilitating it further with every turn. Jouissance drifts away from pleasure, and the system can eventually become addicted to jouissance itself, indifferent to pleasure. Perversion.

A virtuous circuitry is the human machine's original template, created to specification to produce more energy than it discards, a surplus of ongoing loops within primary structures that regularly cleanse and refine both data and energetic substrate. The original template is made to process energy and complex information in vast quantities. A cure, then, is a work of restoration: a return to the original human template. The goal of the system is to release energy, but not in any old way. It has to be pleasurable, a perfect correspondence between charge and container. A proportional, symmetrical, harmonic relationship. A click. This is the pass, in psychoanalysis: the "bolt of lightning" that runs through the patient like a machine being switched on.

In a virtuous circuit, the MB provides a wrapper, an adequate container, that reflects with precision the emotional tone and the energy charge held in the packet delivered by the EB. This requires the MB to take the time, do the work, and have the right tools to unpack the information, analyze it, and reassemble it as it sees fit. The mechanism between EB and MB works as a mirror. If the alphanumeric string finds no sensory anchor, it collapses. If the physical body finds no verbal vehicle, it collapses. Both systems need to signal each other in synchronicity and balance, providing the right support for the other, a kind of mutual empathy or harmonizing.

The "dysregulated nervous system" is the consequence of a differential between these two inputs, when one fails to meet the other. For health to exist, for homeostasis, the living equilibrium, the primary circuit has to be tended: the one that gets the mirrored, exact correspondence right between the data of the PB, the information of the EB, and the synthesis of the MB. This is achieved by removing all interference, corruption, contamination, or distortion, through alignment and coherence: facing things as they are, maintaining concordance between actions (PB), feelings (EB), and words (MB), and correcting as required so as to minimize friction, entropy, *effort*. Because the opposite of effort is *rest*.

Every new experience brings new energy. Familiarity and comfort zone produce decreasing marginal gains. That is why we die. Not breakdown, just... diminishing return until stillness.

But efficiency has a cost: the container shapes what it can hold. A worn signifier filters the incoming experience through its own history. You stop seeing the thing and start seeing the category.

So the full arc of the packet:

Familiarity as decreasing marginal return is precise. The known costs less to process — which means it also yields less. The circuit runs cheaper and emptier simultaneously.

The cure being implicit in the model: novelty, genuine encounter, experiences that rupture the existing signifier library and force new containers. Boldness, the willingness to face the genuinely new, is the mechanism that keeps the marginal gain from approaching zero. New experiences bring new energy. New containers expand the library. # The Cycle and Mental Illness

Psychotherapy, talk therapy especially, tries to create a gap, a hiatus, where to suspend the inertial, traumatic narrative, and thus build a new verbal string capable of processing the accumulated physical-emotional energy into a structured release. Some call this process *the pass*.

The verbal string built by the Mental Body must match, exactly, the information contained in the packet presented by the Emotional Body, in order for the energetic charge to be released successfully onto the PB, and the signifier to be stored correctly in the Memory Library.

This instance, the penetration of the alphanumeric key into the emotional lock, is the critical core operation for a structured release.

In a toxic circuit, the MB accesses the traumatic verbal chain, the master key, to penetrate the EB's packet and deflate it, purge it. This generates a painful, unconscious, and seemingly compulsive catharsis, since it happens involuntarily.

This process runs at the back of consciousness, which is overwhelmed, and therefore presents as incomprehensible, unintelligible, unacceptable to the system.

Then the circuit restarts.

A partial, unstructured release has occurred, but the original packet regenerates, searching for its correct, definitive resolution. With every iteration the packet grows stronger, more aggressive, a nightmarish Terminator, because it is trying to break through the barrier the MB keeps raising against it. Eventually it becomes unrecognizable, further and further from its original form, making it harder and harder to find the exact alphanumeric string to release it.

Talk therapy tries, in a fairly rudimentary way, to provide tools for building a verbal container that serves the patient. Its success rate, though, is low: it rarely manages to drain the energy accumulated over years of ad hoc and secondary circuits, stagnant packets, leaks, and so forth.

If we stopped pathologizing these imbalances and paid attention to their mechanics instead, it would become evident that all mental disorders and chronic conditions run on the principle of cyclicity, and that their disarray informs us about the nature and characteristics of that particular distortion. This holds for isolated symptoms as much as for chronic or severe conditions, from ADHD and autism to bipolar disorder, psychosis, complex post-traumatic stress, borderline, and others.
