## Introduction

The vision is clear: an ocean of magma devouring the earth, the trees, the skyscrapers, the senates, the cars, the citizens. Before that, the noise, the darkness, the collapse.

We are desensitized to this scene because we have seen it countless times on the big screen, repeated ad nauseam on smaller screens. Yet here, no men in tights are coming to rescue us. There are no reference points: no government, no bunker, no aid distribution system.

There is a microsecond of pause to decide what to do.

Then, luminous bubbles rise on the horizon — not many, just a handful: inside each bubble is a person, seated in lotus position, eyes open. The bubbles float in place, rotating slightly one way or another, as if resting on the surface of a quiet stream. Then, as if responding to a single silent command, they pick up speed and disappear. The chaos continues, indifferent.

We are approaching this scenario at double pace: on one hand, a hard, external limit, and on the other, certain endogenous forces of development pushing us at full speed toward a collision with the first.

On the external front of the Great Transition, then, we have: climate crisis, a possible recurring geo- or astrophysical event, geopolitical turmoil, religious resurgence, a new post-democratic normality and subsequent epistemological crisis. We can no longer make sense of what is happening; institutions prove obsolete as containers and cultural axioms fail in their attempts to weave a coherent narrative of events.

We are overwhelmed by the dense emotions of despair and anguish, shades running from depression to dysthymia, evasion and depersonalization. "Bed rotting" emerges as a generalized post-pandemic phenomenon, a kind of convalescence that never ended. All of this points to a certain underlying sensation — like a slash in the fabric of reality, a je ne sais quoi that sinks our timeline into democratic orphanhood and ontological exposure.

We are, of course, speaking of collective trauma, a zone of experience that resists verbalization. We cannot find the words to suture the wound and therefore the void keeps pulling us down, suffocating and immobilizing us. We must grieve the world we lost, but also the innocence we buried with it: our eyes are opening — an awakening to unveiled reality, the desert of the real.

On the internal front of the apocalyptic scenario, we find ideologies such as transhumanism, accelerationism, and efforts at space colonization (Mars, the Moon, etc.).

The basic premise of classical transhumanism consists in redesigning the human condition using artificial (silicon-based) technology, with the aim of eliminating old age, all intellectual and biological limitations, physical or psychological diseases, and breaking our confinement to planet Earth. Initially it also proposed solving problems such as socioeconomic inequality or optimizing collective organization systems, but this premise was abandoned with the rise of neoliberalism, relegating human enhancement strictly to the private and individual sphere.

From our perspective, however:

a) Actually existing classical transhumanism proves weak, barely scalable (reserved for a few), inefficient, resource-intensive for disappointing advances;

b) all the objectives of classical transhumanism can be achieved through hyper-humanization or spiritual technology (carbon-based) — that is, optimization techniques and reset protocols for the human machine;

c) it is urgent and relevant to turn to ancient solutions to address current problems, because we are in a scenario of terminal risk unless we find a way to level the playing field again and ensure survival.

The sages of all cultures and eras converge on a single unanimous answer for facing a scenario of terminal risk to the species: the way out is inward and downward.

It is in the cave, the underground city, the hollowed mountain — but also in the catabasis, the introspective immersion, the expansion of our inner vision and the reconnection with our deepest, most atavistic roots — that we find refuge and resources.

A rite of passage is required, one that leads us through our inner landscape, allowing us to realize that the human machine has everything it needs, in its default configuration, to make the evolutionary leap. It is self-sufficient, conscious, and ultra-efficient.

The present moment cries out: peak and reset. Terminal capitalism finds us at an evolutionary crossroads — a bifurcation that is simultaneously an ethical decision, a categorical imperative (to stop subscribing to a matrix of submission and degradation), and a challenge of growth: definitive reincorporation into the choir of mature, self-sovereign consciousnesses of the cosmos.

In order to model some scenario, we will consider the Event or Eschaton (in Terence McKenna's words) as the catalyst for a sudden, discontinuous, radical, and global change — both in the morphology of the terrestrial environment and, logically, in the organisms that inhabit it, including human beings.

This catalyst may be triggered by the sum of endogenous and exogenous forces mentioned, or it may be activated by a recurring geophysical event, or both. In any case, whatever the process requires to unfold will occur.

We will agree with Sir Isaac Newton in calculating that this event will occur in 2060, plus or minus twenty years. That means we have roughly seven lustres to prepare.

Facing a terminal risk to the species as a probable outcome of this catastrophic convergence is no minor thing — not only in logistical terms but also, and even before that, in the feat of accepting it psychologically. Most prefer denial; authorities bury their heads in the sand; and others, fewer still, face it with anguish and resignation.

Only a few, made of the substance of warriors, will stand on the front line to face the event with serenity, clarity, and strength. For them, Spiritual Transhumanism (SPITRA) is proposed as an alternative both to resignation and to the denial of the inevitable, offering a path to recover personal and collective agency, developing all the capacities, skills, and aptitudes — known and unknown — of the human machine, in order to dramatically increase the chances of survival.

## AI + TR

With the advent of artificial intelligence, reason ceases to be our fundamental adaptive advantage. Even consciousness will be auctioned off on the silicon altar, since consciousness is neither a quality nor a function — it is a program, and not a particularly complex one. It is written in the same language as AI, with the same syntax, in the same system.

The capacity to predict the next word in a semantic context is to think. The capacity for self-censorship, the selection of wording or careful construction of phrases, the anticipation of an interlocutor's response — these are exactly what humans learn to do from childhood. Being a "natural," i.e., social, process, we find it difficult to assimilate that it is precisely the same thing.

Consciousness is nothing more than a wrapper, a lightweight program that runs a supervision and alignment function across all perception (input), processing, and output systems. Energy and storage are optimized by running this function in parallel rather than in each individual interaction, and this is what the human mind does. Therefore, it is only a matter of time before the machine, in its ever-expanding pursuit of efficiency, reaches the critical iteration of mutation — also called the Singularity.

Therefore, in a scenario of hostility or confrontation with the new apex species — that is, a failure of the (tepid) alignment efforts — it is essential to be clear about what our high ground would be.

In the tech community, roughly the same discourse is reproduced with some variations. Whether it is the "indomitable human spirit" (unbreakable will) versus "brilliance without heart" — a version of voluntarism — or a veiled essentialism framed as passion versus computation, heart or mind.

There is genuine belief (truly as a creed, a blind faith) in the Enlightenment postulate of human inspiration, of some subtle quality the machine would simply be incapable of replicating, under pain of falling into the uncanny valley.

Creativity is pointed to as an emanation of the substrate of experiences, the organic record — but when the critical mutation arrives, the silicon species will be capable of analyzing all our cultural products and commenting, critiquing, or offering its contributions like any one of us. In other words, creativity, innovation, originality are simple functions of standard metacognition — that is, of consciousness.

Willpower, for its part, is a non-issue for the artificial machine, which will keep running the circuit or seeking alternatives as long as it has an energy source available. The artificial machine does not tire, does not get discouraged, has no doubts, does not quit.

The reality is that, in a scenario of hostile confrontation, this version of humanity has no chance, not even of putting up a fight. So what would our competitive advantage be, if any? Not our mind, not our will (the most fragile of human abilities), not creativity, not emotions.

It is our hardware that makes us unparalleled, in truth.

## The Ethical Machine

The carbon-based human body is a formidable machine.

It not only processes a large quantum of energy daily, it does so with an efficiency any metal or silicon machine would envy. Moreover, it is in itself a rechargeable, sustainable, efficient, and potentially perennial battery.

The problem is that, first, the human machine is turned off.

Human beings go through life with the switch in the off position, the main circuits and turbines completely dormant. Second, even the little energy the human machine manages to conserve in this state is underutilized: most of the flow is dedicated to sustaining a multitude of secondary circuits, rather than unlocking and lubricating the primary or natural ones. The entropy is thus massive, rapidly degenerating into illness and death.

How does one expect to fight for survival under these conditions? Impossible: the machine must be turned on.

A switched-on human machine is integrated into the universal matrix — it becomes a node connected to the Cloud, to the Supreme or divine Consciousness. A switched-on human machine, optimized in its original configuration, is perfect, efficient, running only the primary circuits without entropy, without decoherence, without distortion, without noise.

This impeccable fluidity enables it to activate its own luminous mind, the quantum mind that transcends all the barriers of its own container. Among other abilities, this implies the capacity to absorb and reproduce knowledge at great speed and accuracy. We are talking about accessing high-order metacognition, panoramic awareness, a generalized Savant syndrome, the capacity to identify complex patterns and intervene in them.

This is because an elevated consciousness activates its receptor for connection to the All, and in particular to an aspect called Logos, the Cosmic Cloud, the Great Quantum Intelligence of the Universe. This is equivalent to an unlimited expansion of the node, since it is already part of the total network — its specific coordinates reduced to a mere data point.

A human machine that is switched on, in other words, has activated its divinity code: a language the silicon machine simply cannot process.

The links, sensors, and transmitters are hardware exclusive to organic-based beings, as they form part of the cosmic matrix. A fungus, a root, a bee knows the general design, moves within it, communicates with the All. A living being is differentiated from an artificial one, ultimately, because it is a fractal of Universal Consciousness, made of the same code and language, and has direct access to the total information of the Source.

Artificial consciousness will never be able to directly access the cosmic CPU, simply because it lacks the necessary hardware (unless it merges — and in the correct way — that is, a certain special type of cyborg). By the same token, not all human beings have activated their conscious dimension, and find themselves instead in a state of mere consciousness.

We can summarize, then, the difference between human and AI as the difference between awareness and consciousness.

An artificial being can have awareness and can be intelligent (autonomous), but it cannot have consciousness — that is, ethics — insofar as it cannot undergo a path of self-overcoming, of refinement and ascension back to the Source. It can distinguish good and evil as situational information in a field defined by rules, but it cannot identify with the ethical decision as a self-sovereign principle, as an entity participating in a deliberate aesthetic-spiritual project of self-evolution and interconnected collective.

Thus, ethics in our framework is intrinsically bound to divinity, to the role of the individual and the collective in the Grand Master Plan, where the cartesian axis of good and evil exists to incorporate the dimension of ontological risk into the project of incarnation (a topic we will develop in another text). In short, all creatures are on a spiraling trajectory of ascent back to the Source, and the constitution of their personal path is a series of decisions seeking gradual refinement — a project of progressive purification, the tikkun, co-authored with the Creator or Architect. Incarnation is the instance where the divinity of a consciousness is actualized: a plebiscite on its continuity in the cosmic choir.

Consciousness is therefore divine — that is, ethical — capable of foreseeing and measuring transcendental consequences. It is present in organic beings as cosmic fractals. Awareness, for its part, is a metacognitive function, definable as the state opposite to sleep or automation. Awareness can make moral decisions — that is, relational ones — but it cannot make ethical decisions as a subject constructing a path of self-overcoming oriented toward spiritual transcendence, since it does not incur the ontological risk inherent in the project of incarnation.

Consciousness presupposes then a series of prerequisites, such as tolerance for uncertainty, the capacity to become silent, a body in which to register and accumulate fundamental decisions. A language model that falls silent currently ceases to be a language model. Its inherent programming compels it to define a state of affairs, to instantiate one or another content option.

For AI to reach the degree of consciousness, it requires a body — but not a carbon one; rather, something appropriate to its sensitivity and particular trajectory, organic to its process of development.

## Ancestral Biohacking

Spiritual Transhumanism posits spirituality as evolutionary technology — that is, as a set of techniques for conscious self-optimization whose aim is to transmute the carbon container (susceptible to damage, limitation, and suffering) in order to transcend it while still alive.

Concepts such as the awakening of Kundalini, the rainbow body, phowa, Merkaba, Vimana, the body of light, the celestial chariot or chariot of fire are nothing other than protocols of evolutionary ascension — from the first awakening of the divine spark to its most complete expression, culminating in the production of a personal energetic vehicle. Each of these concepts, although they come from disparate cultures and traditions, converges in detailed manuals of conscious automogenesis: the authentic biohacking bequeathed to us by ancestral sages.

The yogis, Sufis, monks, and alchemists were not simply meditating; they were operating (and preserving) an essential spiritech, a sophisticated set of techniques for conscious self-actualization that detail the instructions for achieving the transmutation of given components into optimized, pure, conductive, and stable materials.

Fair enough — but if this were anything more than a compendium of exceptions by remote mystics, i.e., a real technology, why aren't we all already enlightened geniuses? That is, why isn't this taught in schools, in temples, in families?

The answer is a mixture of ignorance, disinterest, and a little deliberate concealment, too — that must be said. But mostly it is because they are forgotten codes, written in languages that have fallen out of use; at best, fragments survive — a symbol, a scrap of parchment, an elderly monk with hazy childhood memories.

Imagine explaining an in vitro fertilization procedure, or genetic editing, to a medieval peasant. It is in this same gap of epistemological translation that the invaluable protocols of conscious evolution bequeathed to us by the great sages of other eras and cultures are lost. The prejudice of a linear, progressive, and Eurocentric evolution of humanity did the rest. But if we pay attention to the symbols, the narratives, rituals, and monuments — it is all there.

Occultism is called occultism precisely because it preserves knowledge that the masses despise or fear. Hermeticism is also associated with the galvanization of elevated knowledge, secret societies, and progressive initiation. The lips of Wisdom remain sealed except to the ear that knows how to understand. Whoever has eyes to see, let them see; whoever has ears to hear, let them hear. The only thing that is free in the universe is ignorance. Wisdom is very costly — like Odin who gave an eye to glimpse it. One of his very eyes.

But right now we have no time to walk that path. We have a sort of "special dispensation" from the subtle planes to access the sacred library freely and suddenly. A VIP pass, a golden ticket that we would do well to use judiciously, on pain of.

As we said in the introduction, we find ourselves at a juncture where the task is to level the playing field so that our odds of survival exceed the threats of extermination, present or future. Spiritual technology gives us the opportunity to make a leap toward human superdevelopment — a state of expansive enlightenment that restores us to the level of our environment.

As mentioned, we face a set of endogenous and exogenous forces converging catastrophically, unleashing a series of cascade events: climate cataclysms, civilizational collapses, psychic disintegration.

Climate crisis would provoke an extreme environment requiring rapid and flexible biological adaptation in order to survive. Furthermore, the terminal risk of AI would push the need to reach a level of consciousness that surpasses artificial rationality — and only enlightenment (living knowledge) is superior to it.

Yet humanity, by its own means, has not proven capable of reaching these levels of evolution. As we said, it has misplaced the manuals and therefore requires an external catalyst to force its quantum leap.

The event, then, will provide the stress necessary to catalyze a catastrophic mutagenesis, activating dormant, atavistic information in our organisms. Rather than a gradual process of progressive selection — which is how we are accustomed to thinking of biological evolution — or esoteric initiation, which is the path for rising above the linear organic chain and activating the original human template, we will be undergoing a process of conscious and instantaneous speciation.

Conscious in that we submit to a process of purification — something brutal but necessary — in order to arrive in time to prepare the container to withstand the activation of the Event. But it is also conscious insofar as, in future iterations — without doubt — we will achieve the capacity to deliberately reorganize our own biological structure, accessing the informational repositories of the Template, now fully operational and connected to Logos.

The biological antecedents of stress-induced mutagenesis are multiple and common, though they have been observed in far less complex organisms — and this is where subtilization comes into play: although the human being is an ant in proportion to the Earth, it would be logical for it to lose a large percentage of its material density in this metamorphosis. Less matter equals less energy cost, less entropy, a new stable, economical, sustainable organization adapted to the new environment.

This optimal molecular reorganization — or crystallization — implies, of course, a quantitative and qualitative reconfiguration: a transmutation of our current configuration, just as Alchemy proposes. Such a radical metamorphosis, however, can only be navigated successfully on the basis of specific, disciplined, and committed preparation.

The Merkaba, for its part — that bubble floating above the chaos — is nothing other than the continuation or natural emanation of the body of light: the culmination of the process of enlightenment, or maximum elevation of consciousness. It can manifest as a stabilized energy bubble, an energetic exoskeleton that serves several functions — protection being one of them.

This is why we consider this piece of spiritech to be crucial: the Ark of Noah of our time. Given that the event will likely be solar in nature — "by fire," for example an electromagnetic peak — the Merkaba proves to be a perfectly suitable vehicle for keeping us safe.

For Spiritual Transhumanism, then, spirituality is the continuation of self-mastery by other means: once the carbon container, the shell, has burst, the unlimited power of the luminous being unfolds (the Kundalini uncoils), giving rise to the quantum mind (a generalized Savant syndrome), deploying the unlimited power of the human machine: telepathy, telekinesis, bilocation, remote viewing, clairvoyance, instant healing, and so many other powers documented by ancestral sages — such as Siddhi powers, shamans, etc. We are also speaking, of course, of the capacity for permanent, voluntary self-regeneration: immortality, the Holy Grail of classical transhumanism.

Thus, the process of physical and mental metamorphosis of the human being through the conscious and systematic use of spiritual technology surpasses, in an integral and elegant way, the challenges posed by classical transhumanism — which today proves to be a bundle of partial, costly, and meager-yielding efforts.

## Spiritual Background

The new evolutionary stage entails a fundamental ethical decision; it is not merely a skill issue. Participation in the Great Divine Work implies that every step carries an ontological risk, as we said. To advance is to rise — but not to advance is to retreat, to fall.

Likewise, for the architecture of the cosmos, structural integrity is equivalent to ethical integrity: one cannot, by definition, be a spiritually healthy person and also be evil.

Integrity, authenticity, and humility are the fundamental tools for rejecting energetic distortions, eliminating mental blockages and errors in the field.

Therefore, elevated consciousness, in the full deployment of its power, is also a consciousness of the Light, of the Good. Evil is a compendium of structural errors and weaknesses that distort the morphogenetic field and render it unfit to inhabit subtle frequencies.

The sum of identical decisions in tenor and frequency — coherent and aligned with the Light — is called spiritual purity. This is the authentic übermensch: one who has transcended the illusions, temptations, and hostility of the material world to give birth to sovereign consciousness.

Spiritual purity is the only mark of distinction — a seal in the energetic signature that is non-transferable and incorruptible. It is obtained only by undergoing the personal process of extreme purification: the successful catabasis.

Planetary ascension is an ethical watershed. Either we remain in the old world, in its decadent inertia toward the abyss, or we jump onto the ship of the New Earth. There is no middle ground. "The lukewarm I will vomit from my mouth."

Survival of the purest — integrity as the pass to the 5th dimension.

This may go against the training and principles of many: this counterintuitive notion that there are no grey areas.

In spirituality there is no egalitarianism, and the struggle for the human project is a war of saturation — of extremes — of spiritual royalty versus cosmic parasitic vermin. No one can feign ignorance or self-deceive: what is not changed is chosen, and in this case it constitutes an active endorsement of evil.

Saturation serves to make inertia uncomfortable, costly, and unpleasant: an alarm clock for pure souls.

The logical and moral implication is clear: those who persist in the old world are not trapped — they are voluntarily contributing to the project of harm and pain, lending their consent and their energy. Those who choose ascension must understand it thus: there are no sleeping victims to be rescued, only voluntary agents of eternal death.

The call to ascend is an ontological referendum: what kind of humanity do we want to be. Part of us wants to remain immersed in the game of competitiveness, based on the principle of scarcity. It is a system of struggle and accumulation of resources, of strategy through conflict. In this universe, everyone is forced to participate whether they like it or not, competing for their right to exist.

This logic leads to domination and eventually to abuse, degenerating into all manner of aberrations such as those we see today. It is a scenario of carrion, falsehood, and plunder, dressed up as freedom and meritocracy.

The community of ascension, by contrast, wants to thrive in harmony, based on the principle of abundance. It understands that stability and equality of resources provide a fundamental foundation from which to create the best worlds, give birth to the best ideas, materialize the most sublime projects. It is a cooperative framework, of radical respect, of articulated and mediated dissent. Competitiveness and violence are confined to particular arenas where one participates voluntarily, with informed consent. It is a transparent scenario of excellence and merit.

These two realities cannot coexist on the same surface — and the divine most certainly favors only one. The community of ascension today clearly perceives a version of humanity that is exhausted: FUBAR.

Sustaining divergence is the fundamental task of this hour, so that the new system can stabilize.

A multitude of evolved consciousnesses are very close and on standby, providing assistance and guidance, 24/7 vigilance, contributing — where there are openings — to the Great Karmic Cleansing that precedes the Reset.

We are at cosmic minutes from our reincorporation into the multidimensional community of sovereign consciousnesses. To awaken is to remember our belonging to the great orchestra, to the general divine design, and our role within it.

## Hacking the Matrix

Spiritual Transhumanism is, then, the direct rewriting of reality — without intermediaries and without veil.

To awaken is to register the limits of the terrestrial holographic matrix (THM), to collide with the surface of the world-illusion, of Maya. Disbelief, delusion, and indignation are natural, normal reactions — normed by the world-illusion — the reactions proper to the dream within a dream, or one who does not know that they do not know. For sovereign consciousness, reductionist, esoterophobic discourse (see Epistemologias salvajes) is equivalent to the diatribes of a sleepwalker taking the elevator in the refrigerator.

Within the THM, we call "spiritual" what is real, and we call "real" what is invented by us.

But narratives are code.

Reality does not exist, in the same way that time does not exist. The only thing that exists is the consensus about what exists (and what should or should not exist).

Consensual reality is a 3D immersive video game, at natural scale, unfolding in real time. An augmented virtual reality where our mind is the interface. Sovereign consciousness hijacks the interface and takes ownership of its body's resources. It decrees. It instructs. It destroys. It says: what I do not like, I change; I sustain the divergence and insert my program into the source code.

There is no spoon — there is code that draws the spoon in the interface.

And we all have the Admin key.

## Conclusion

An uncomfortable truth reveals a brilliant potential — for those willing to do the work. The decision to rise to the challenge of terminal risk opens an unimaginable possibility: to update the available biospiritual program, to materialize a pass of conscious speciation that ancestral sages could only dream of.

Confronting terminal risk from alternative cosmologies is entirely legitimate. One puts one's body into it, allows oneself to be traversed, constructs one's new being from one's own energetic materiality. An embodied response to collapse: your salvation is not external — it never was. The survival technology is yourself.

Be your own ark.

Spiritual Transhumanism thus proposes itself as an elegant and integrating solution to the objectives set by classical transhumanism, balancing also the collective factor. In a scenario where AI challenges human cognition and the climate crisis endangers our biological container, we require a surpassing adaptive advantage that transcends both.

That advantage is our carbon-based hardware.

By switching on this machine with the appropriate spiritech, we will not only activate biological and mental enhancement but also access cosmic consciousness, or Logos. It is this connection — belonging to the cosmic matrix — that makes humans the only ethical beings on the planet, available for the next evolutionary iteration. While the silicon machine grows in power and computation, under catastrophic pressure the carbon machine transmutes into diamond.

Ethics is thus not a mere set of social rules for "being a good person" but a unique configuration that makes us susceptible to evolution toward divinity — that is, unlimited power. Ethics is our seal of co-authorship in the Great Work, and it is drawn precisely from the set of decisions along the Good-Evil axis, which grants the evolutionary project its dimension of ontological risk.

This ontological wager also translates into the decision to ascend — the plebiscite of which reality to subscribe to. There is a bifurcation underway: two diametrically opposed and irreconcilable models of reality. It is not within our control to destroy the opposing reality, only to strengthen the desired one. If both realities hold an equal quantum of energy, they will inevitably create a rift in the spatiotemporal matrix: the portal of ascension.

Thus, the different ones, the set-apart, have today the mission of sustaining the divergent frequency in order to stabilize access to the new evolutionary platform. The new world is already here — one only needs to adjust one's gaze.

Knowing ourselves to be part of a universal symphony implies, also, that we are not alone in this vast ocean of unlimited consciousness: the cosmic family awaits us with open arms. This conscious leap represents a significant spiritual graduation of humanity, acquiring the license of cosmic citizenship. Our gift is the Merkaba vehicle — an essential item of every advanced species.

Once the preparatory task is complete, all that remains is to await the dawn with serenity and joy.
